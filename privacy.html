hungriAR CRM

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

hungriAR ("we", "us") runs the hungriAR CRM at crm.hungriar.app. Restaurants and cafes ("restaurants") use it to keep a list of their guests, send them WhatsApp messages, collect ratings, and manage coupons and loyalty rewards. This policy explains what personal data the service handles, why, and the choices you have.

1. Who is responsible for your data

If you are a guest of a restaurant, that restaurant decides what to do with your data, and we process it on its behalf. If you are a restaurant owner or staff member using the CRM, hungriAR is responsible for your account data.

2. Data we handle

  • Guests: mobile number, name (if given), birthday (if given), visit dates, bill amounts recorded at check-in, tags added by the restaurant, coupons issued and redeemed, star ratings and written feedback, whether you agreed to WhatsApp messages or unsubscribed, and the delivery status of messages sent to you (sent, delivered, read). If you join by scanning a QR code, we also receive the name on your WhatsApp profile.
  • Restaurant accounts: business name, login ID, a securely hashed password, WhatsApp Business connection details, and settings.
  • Technical data: our hosting providers keep standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and troubleshooting.

We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. The dashboard stores a login session in the browser only to keep staff signed in.

3. Why we use it

  • To let a restaurant recognise returning guests, record visits and apply loyalty rewards.
  • To send WhatsApp messages you agreed to receive: offers, coupon codes, birthday treats, a welcome message, and a request to rate your visit.
  • To pass your rating and feedback to the restaurant so it can improve.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

Messages are sent only to guests who gave consent at the restaurant, by scanning the restaurant's QR code, or by messaging the restaurant on WhatsApp. We do not sell personal data.

4. Who we share it with

  • The restaurant you visited. Restaurants can only see their own guests, never another restaurant's.
  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform), which delivers WhatsApp messages and tells us whether they were delivered or read. Meta's own privacy policy applies to WhatsApp.
  • Our infrastructure providers: Supabase (database) and Vercel (hosting), which store and process data on our behalf.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Stop messages: reply STOP to any message from the restaurant. You will not receive further offers. Reply START to join again.
  • Access, correction or deletion: ask the restaurant directly, or write to us at the address below and tell us the restaurant name and your mobile number.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may also raise a grievance with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India.

6. Data deletion

To have your data deleted, email hungriar.app@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request", the restaurant's name, and your mobile number. We will confirm the request and delete your guest record, including visits, coupons, ratings and message history, for that restaurant. A restaurant can also delete a guest from its dashboard at any time. When a restaurant closes its account, its guest data is deleted.

7. How long we keep data

We keep guest data while the restaurant uses the service, or until the guest or restaurant asks us to delete it. Server logs are kept for a short period by our hosting providers.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Each restaurant can only access its own data, and webhook messages from WhatsApp are checked for Meta's signature. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify affected parties of a breach as the law requires.

9. Children

The service is not meant for children under 18. Restaurants should not add children's details.
